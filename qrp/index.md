# QRP & Homebrew Radio — A Resource Directory

*A growing, curated directory of QRP (low-power ham radio) and homebrew-electronics resources —
references, kits, schematics, articles, videos, parts sources, and notes worth keeping. Published
from Constellation `pages/qrp/` via GitHub Pages.*

*Maintained by AJ6YV. Started 2026-08-10 · first curation pass 2026-08-13.*

> **How this grows:** resources land here **each week during inbox processing** — when Bill emails
> himself a QRP link/idea (or one surfaces in the notes), it gets added here and the page is pushed.
> A living list, not a one-time build.

## Local — Santa Cruz County *(start here)*
You're in Scotts Valley, and QRP has real gravity right here:

- **[Elecraft](https://elecraft.com/)** — the legendary QRP kit maker (K1, K2, KX2, KX3) is based in
  **Watsonville**, essentially in your backyard. Their kits are the gold standard for learn-by-building
  transceivers, and being local is worth leveraging.
- **[SCCARC — Santa Cruz County ARC (K6BJ)](https://k6bj.org/)** — the county club: regular meetings,
  repeaters, Field Day, an active membership. The default home club for the area.
- **[SLVARC — San Lorenzo Valley ARC (WR6AOK)](https://www.slvarc.org/)** — the closer, smaller club
  between San Jose and Santa Cruz. Repeater **147.120 MHz (+600, 94.8 Hz)**; mailing address PO Box 455,
  Felton. Welcomes anyone regardless of residency.
- **[Pacificon](https://www.pacificon.org/)** — the ARRL Pacific Division convention (San Ramon, CA),
  a day-trip away, with QRP/homebrew forums and a builders' presence.

## Organizations & Community
- **[QRP ARCI](https://qrparci.org/)** — QRP Amateur Radio Club International. Publishes *QRP Quarterly*,
  runs sprints and awards, and puts on **FDIM** (below). The main US QRP body — worth joining.
- **[G-QRP Club](https://www.gqrp.com/)** — the UK QRP club; its journal *SPRAT* is a homebrew treasure.
- **NorCal QRP Club** — the historic California QRP club behind many classic minimalist kits; good archive
  of designs even where activity has quieted.
- **Mailing lists (groups.io):** [QRP-L](https://groups.io/g/QRP-L) (the main QRP list) ·
  [QRPp](https://groups.io/g/QRPp) (extreme low power) · [kitsandparts](https://groups.io/g/kitsandparts)
  (toroids/RF parts + the toroids.info calculator).

## Books & References
- **ARRL Handbook** — the general reference (vintage 1965–85 editions are especially good for discrete/QRP).
- **Experimental Methods in RF Design (EMRFD)** — Hayward (W7ZOI), Campbell, Larkin. The modern homebrew
  bible; buy this first.
- **Solid State Design for the Radio Amateur (SSDRA)** — Hayward/DeMaw. The predecessor classic.
- **Introduction to RF Design** — Wes Hayward (W7ZOI).
- **W1FB's QRP Notebook / Design Notebook** — Doug DeMaw. Practical, buildable circuits.
- **The Joy of QRP** — Adrian Weiss (W0RSP). The philosophy + minimalist operating.
- **Hands-On Radio Experiments** — Ward Silver (N0AX). Bite-sized circuit lessons.
- **Journals:** *QRP Quarterly* (QRP ARCI) · *SPRAT* (G-QRP) · *QST* archives.

## Kits & Designs
- **[QRP Labs](https://qrp-labs.com/)** — Hans Summers. The **QCX/QCX-mini** CW transceiver and **QMX**
  multiband — extraordinary capability per dollar, superb build docs. The best modern learn-by-building entry.
- **[Elecraft](https://elecraft.com/)** — K1 / KX2 / KX3 / K2. Premium kits, local (Watsonville).
- **[4 State QRP Group](https://www.4sqrp.com/)** — NS-40, Cricket, Bayou Jumper; friendly, low-cost club kits.
- **[Pacific Antenna / QRPkits.com](https://qrpkits.com/)** — receivers, transceivers, and accessory kits.
- **[QRPme](https://www.qrpme.com/)** — Rex W1REX. Altoids-tin "Tuna Tin"–style fun; great for a weekend build.
- **[BITX / µBITX / sBITX](https://www.hfsignals.com/)** — Farhan (VU2ESE). Open-source SSB transceivers;
  the community design that launched a thousand homebrews.

## Articles & Writeups
- **[QRPer.com](https://qrper.com/)** — Thomas (K4SWL). Field radio, gear reviews, POTA/SOTA — very active.
- **[SolderSmoke](https://soldersmoke.com/)** — Bill Meara (N2CQR). Homebrew blog + long-running podcast;
  the culture and the "why" of building your own.
- **[ARRL — QRP / Low-Power Operating](https://www.arrl.org/qrp-low-power-operating)** — the primer.

## Videos & Channels
- **[Charlie Morris ZL2CTM](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCSNPW3_gzuMJcX_ErBZTv2g)** — designs QRP rigs
  from a blank sheet, stage by stage. The single best channel for homebrew method.
- **VK3YE (Peter Parker)** — prolific QRP/homebrew and portable-ops videos.
- **W2AEW (Alan Wolke)** — RF and test-gear tutorials; invaluable given you have no metrology bench yet.
- **Ham Radio Workbench** (podcast) — projects, tools, and homebrew news.

## Parts & Suppliers
- **[kitsandparts.com](https://kitsandparts.com/)** — Diz (W8DIZ). Toroids, RF parts, the "Toroid King,"
  and the QRP Erector Set. Pair with the **[toroids.info](https://toroids.info/)** winding calculator.
- **Mini-Circuits** — mixers and RF modules (you already scrounge these — e.g. LRFMS-4).
- **Mouser / DigiKey** — general semis and passives (NE602/SA612, op-amps, crystals).
- **Tayda Electronics** — cheap passives and enclosures for prototyping.
- **DX Engineering / GigaParts** — radios, antenna wire, connectors, coax.

## Events
- **FDIM (Four Days In May)** — QRP ARCI's conference at Dayton Hamvention; the QRP homebrew highlight of
  the year (you've attended and scrounged RF parts there).
- **Pacificon** — regional (San Ramon, CA), with a QRP/homebrew track.
- **Field Day** (June) — via SCCARC/SLVARC; low-power categories reward QRP.

## Operating — where QRP shines
- **[POTA — Parks On The Air](https://parksontheair.com/)** and **[SOTA — Summits On The Air](https://www.sota.org.uk/)**
  — portable, low-power activations; the fastest way to actually *use* a rig you built and rack up contacts.

## SDR / receivers (researching)
*Bill's shortlist, Aug 2026 — from self-sent notes.*
- **Airspy HF+ / HF+ Discovery** — a well-regarded HF/VHF SDR receiver for weak-signal HF. See **[sdrswitch.com](https://sdrswitch.com/)**.
- **SDR Console (SDRConsole)** — popular Windows SDR receiver software; pairs with the Airspy.
